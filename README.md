# IVF After 40: What to Know

A quick guide to fertility testing, egg quality and IVF planning for women over 40.

> ⚠️ **Medical note:** This guide is for general education only. Talk to a fertility specialist about your own situation.

---

## Is IVF After 40 Possible?

**Yes**, but it needs a detailed, personalised evaluation. Fertility declines with age, so ASRM recommends that **women over 40 get evaluated and treated sooner** rather than waiting.

Age isn't the only factor. Doctors also look at ovarian reserve, menstrual history, past pregnancies, previous treatment, uterine and tubal health, sperm quality and overall medical history.

---

## Why Fertility Changes After 40

Both the **number and the quality of eggs** decline with age. This can affect:

- How many eggs are retrieved
- Fertilisation and embryo development
- Chromosomal health of embryos
- Miscarriage risk

---

## Ovarian Reserve Tests

| Test | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **AMH** | Egg reserve and likely response to stimulation |
| **Antral Follicle Count** | Ultrasound count of small follicles |
| **FSH & other hormones** | Ordered as needed |

> **AMH is not a pregnancy test.** It predicts egg numbers, not egg quality or IVF success.

---

## Tests Before IVF

Hormone tests, pelvic ultrasound, uterine and tubal assessment, semen analysis, a review of past pregnancies and treatment, and genetic counselling if needed. Testing should fit **your** history.

---

## IVF With Your Own Eggs

Possible after 40, but outcomes depend on age and individual factors.

> The CDC cautions that average success rates **don't represent your personal chance**.

---

## Egg Quality vs. Quantity

With age, more eggs may carry chromosomal abnormalities. You can have a good egg reserve and still have age-related changes in quality, so both need to be checked.

---

## Key Takeaway

IVF after 40 should be **personalised**, not based on age alone.
